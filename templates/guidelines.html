            Dodees is built for adults who want to meet, flirt, connect, and explore real possibilities.

            Be honest. Be respectful. Be safe. If you cannot do that, Dodees may not be the right place for you.


            1. Be Yourself

            Use your real identity and your own photos.

            Do not pretend to be someone else. Do not use stolen pictures. Do not lie about your age, gender, location, relationship intentions, or identity.


            2. Respect Boundaries

            Different people have different comfort levels.

            Do not pressure anyone for replies, photos, videos, private contact details, sex, money, gifts, or meetings.

            Consent matters in every conversation.


            3. Keep Public Content Appropriate

            Public profiles, photos, and Reels should be suitable for a dating community.

            Do not post nudity, sexual content, sexual offers, violent content, hate content, illegal content, or content meant to shock, shame, or harass.


            4. Private Chats Still Have Rules

            Private does not mean anything goes.

            Do not send unwanted sexual content, threats, abuse, scams, spam, or anything intended to intimidate or exploit another person.


            5. No Harassment or Abuse

            Do not bully, insult, stalk, threaten, shame, blackmail, expose, or intimidate anyone.

            Do not target people because of their race, tribe, nationality, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, age, appearance, or background.


            6. No Scams or Money Pressure

            Do not ask users for money, OTPs, passwords, cards, bank details, gift cards, crypto, loans, investments, or emergency transfers.

            Do not use romance, urgency, guilt, or fake stories to get money or personal information.


            7. No Minors

            Dodees is for adults only.

            Do not create an account if you are under 18. Do not post or request sexual content involving minors. Any suspected child exploitation may be reported to authorities.


            8. Use Reels and Reveal Responsibly

            Only upload content you have the right to share.

            Do not use Reels, Reveal, photos, or captions to shame, expose, impersonate, mislead, or exploit another person.


            9. Respect AI Features

            Dodees may offer AI-assisted agents and suggestions. Do not use these features to mislead, manipulate, harass, impersonate, or pressure anyone.


            10. Report Concerns

            If you see something unsafe, fake, abusive, sexual, violent, misleading, or suspicious, report it.

            You can also block or hide users you do not want to interact with.


            11. What Happens If You Break These Rules

            Dodees may warn you, remove content, reject photos or Reels, limit your account, remove verification, suspend your account, ban you, cancel access to features, or take other action.

            Serious or repeated violations may lead to a permanent ban.
