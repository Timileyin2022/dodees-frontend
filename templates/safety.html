            Dating should feel exciting, not unsafe. These tips are here to help you make better choices while using Dodees.


            1. Protect Your Personal Information

            Do not share sensitive information too early, including:

            - home address;
            - workplace address;
            - passwords;
            - OTPs;
            - bank details;
            - card details;
            - government IDs;
            - private documents;
            - financial information.


            2. Do Not Send Money

            Never send money to someone you met on Dodees, even if their story sounds urgent or emotional.

            Be careful with requests for transport money, medical emergencies, business investments, gifts, crypto, loans, or account help.


            3. Stay in the App Until You Trust Them

            Scammers often try to move conversations quickly to other apps.

            Take your time. Use Dodees messages, reports, and blocking tools while you are still getting to know someone.


            4. Watch for Red Flags

            Be careful if someone:

            - refuses to show their face;
            - avoids basic questions;
            - asks for money;
            - asks for OTPs or bank details;
            - declares love too quickly;
            - pressures you to meet privately;
            - sends unwanted sexual content;
            - gives inconsistent stories;
            - tries to isolate you from friends or family.


            5. Meeting Offline

            If you decide to meet someone:

            - meet in a public place;
            - tell a trusted person where you are going;
            - arrange your own transport;
            - keep your phone charged;
            - do not leave your drink unattended;
            - leave if you feel uncomfortable.


            6. Report Unsafe Behavior

            Report users who appear fake, abusive, threatening, sexual, violent, misleading, or suspicious.

            Blocking a user can help you avoid further contact.


            7. Verification Is Helpful, Not Perfect

            Verification, profile review, Reels, and AI suggestions can help improve trust, but they do not guarantee a person's identity, character, intentions, or safety.

            Always use your own judgment.
